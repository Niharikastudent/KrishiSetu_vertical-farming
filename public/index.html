Fair price for your harvest, together as a collective

Instead of selling alone at mandi distress prices, pool harvest with neighbouring farmers. Agree on one minimum price and send verified direct quotes to mills and wholesale buyers — with zero middlemen commissions.

🌾
Crop Pooling (फसल एकत्रीकरण) Combine harvest of the same crop to build wholesale volume (50–500 quintals) attractive to large commercial processors.
⚖️
Unanimous Price Voting (मूल्य सहमति) Vote in private group chat on minimum acceptable rate based on APMC mandi prices and Govt MSP. Every farmer must agree.
🏭
Direct Mill Buyer Outreach (सीधी बिक्री) Send collective quotation directly to verified flour mills, dal processors, and food companies with pickup and quality details.
